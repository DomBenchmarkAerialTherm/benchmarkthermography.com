Recent flights

What we saw, and what we told the owner to do about it.

Business rooftop · Cape Cod

22 panels weren't making power.

What we saw

Two groups of 11 panels were warmer than everything around them. In daylight they look exactly like the rest.

What we told them

Those panels are likely disconnected from the inverter. Have the installer check those two circuits first.

Private residence · Cape Cod

One cell running hot.

What we saw

One small square on one panel was much hotter than anything else on the roof. It showed up from every angle we flew, so it isn't a reflection.

What we told them

A hot spot like this gets worse over time. Have the installer look at that panel while it's still under warranty.

Recent work · Leominster, MA

A roof check for VFW Post 1807

We flew the whole roof for the City of Leominster Veterans' Services: once before sunset for photos, and again after sunset with the thermal camera.

They got a plain-English report showing where water may be getting in, starting with the porch roof, and what to have their roofer check.

VFW Post 1807 · porch roof

A wet spot you can't see from the ground.

What we saw

After sunset, part of the porch roof stayed warm while the rest cooled off. Wet insulation holds heat like that.

What we told them

Have a roofer take a small core sample there to confirm. Fixing it now costs far less than a new roof later.
